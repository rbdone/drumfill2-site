How-to guides

Step-by-step instructions with pictures of the exact keys to tap. New here? Start with Play a song, or install the app first.

  • PlayingEverything on the Play screen. Every key here fires the moment your finger touches it, and nothing you need mid-song is behind a menu.
  • Songs and patternsFind a song, build one in seconds, type one, or edit every bar of it.
  • LiveSetlists, gig mode, pedals and headset buttons for the stage.
  • SoundsSeven kits built in, plus your own kits and Freesound sounds.
  • GearMIDI out and multi-output interfaces, for players with more boxes than friends.
  • BeatboxTurn the groove in your head into drums. Everything is analysed on the phone; nothing is uploaded.
