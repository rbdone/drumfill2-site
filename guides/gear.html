Gear

MIDI out and multi-output interfaces, for players with more boxes than friends.

On this page

  1. MIDI notes out
  2. MIDI clock
  3. Multi-output audio interfaces

MIDI notes out

Play an e-drum module, a synth or a plugin from DrumFill2's grooves.

  1. Settings → MIDI.
  2. Under Send to, plug in a USB MIDI interface and tap Refresh, or Scan Bluetooth for a Bluetooth MIDI device, then pick it.
  3. Sound source: Internal, MIDI (the phone's kit goes quiet) or Both.
  4. Adjust MIDI offset until the module lines up with the phone. The note map (General MIDI by default, channel 10) can be changed per instrument; hi-hat openness can go out as CC4.

MIDI clock

Loopers, delays and drum machines follow DrumFill2's tempo.

  1. In Settings → MIDI, pick the device and switch on Send MIDI clock.
  2. Clock follows the count-in, tempo changes and ritards; Start goes out on the song's first downbeat and Stop on STOP. The status line reports how it's going.

Multi-output audio interfaces

Drums to the PA, click and count-ins to your in-ears.

  1. Connect a USB audio interface with more than two outputs. Settings → Audio & output → Multi-output interface.
  2. Switch on Separate outputs.
  3. Route Drums, Click and Cue voice to 1/2, 3/4, 3, 4 or Off. ▶ plays a test tone on each.
  4. If the phone only opens two channels on your interface, the page says so and DrumFill2 falls back to split mode instead of going silent.

Support varies by phone; four-channel streams can have more latency than stereo.
