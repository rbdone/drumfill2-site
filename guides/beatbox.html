Beatbox

Turn the groove in your head into drums. Everything is analysed on the phone; nothing is uploaded.

On this page

  1. Beatbox a groove
  2. Teach it your sounds
  3. Beatbox Song Builder

Beatbox a groove

2 to 8 bars of “pm-ti-ta-ti” become a drum pattern.

  1. Library → Beatbox. Allow the microphone when asked.
  2. Put headphones on and switch on Click in headphones (without it, the tempo comes from your take). Pick 2, 4 or 8 bars.
  3. ● RECORD and beatbox: “pm” for kick, “ta” for snare, “ti” for hi-hat. ■ STOP when done.
  4. Your hits appear on the step grid and play back with a real kit, with a “sounds like” match from the library. Fix any hit, then make a song from the groove.

Get close to the mic. If it says “No beat heard”, try again a little louder.

Teach it your sounds

Your snare sounds like someone else's hi-hat? Teach it.

  1. On Record Beatbox, tap Teach it your sounds.
  2. For Kick, Snare and Closed hat (Open hat is optional): ● RECORD and make the sound 5 times, about once a second.
  3. Retake your groove. FORGET MY SOUNDS goes back to the general rules.

Beatbox Song Builder

A whole song, one section at a time.

  1. Library → Build a song → scroll down → Build by beatboxing.
  2. Choose a form (Pop, or your own section list), the tempo (Set it now or From my first take), the meter and the kit. START WITH THE INTRO.
  3. Each section shows a hint (“Count in or build up”, “keep it simple”, “bigger, more energy”…). ● RECORD: count along, beatbox, and it stops by itself at the last bar. From the second section on you hear the last 2 bars of the previous one first, so you can play into it.
  4. Keep the take, retake it, edit it or swap in the closest library groove. Repeated sections reuse your take.
  5. On the finish screen, ▶ plays the whole song; tap any section to redo it, then save it to the Library. Progress saves after every kept take, so you can stop and resume.
