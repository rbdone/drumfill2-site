Sounds

Seven kits built in, plus your own kits and Freesound sounds.

The seven built-in kits

Five sampled acoustic kits recorded from real drums, and two generated electronic kits.

  1. Open the Kits tab. Studio (the default), Jazz Club, Vintage, Brushes and Heavy are sampled with several velocity layers and round-robins; Vintage Machine and Lo-Fi are synthesised.
  2. Tap ▶ on a kit to hear it.
  3. Change a song's kit in the Song Editor. Settings → Credits lists every kit's author and licence.

Import an SFZ or Hydrogen kit

Bring your own multisampled kit.

  1. Kits → ↑ (Import kit), then pick a Hydrogen .h2drumkit or a zipped SFZ kit.
  2. The preview shows the kit's name, licence and each detected instrument with ▶. Sounds it couldn't place are in a Needs a slot list: assign them.
  3. IMPORT KIT. The kit editor then lets you play and mix each slot, and shows an import report.

Imported kits are for your own use on your phone; songs you share refer to them by name only.

Browse free kits

A public list of free kits, with licences up front.

  1. Kits → Browse free kits. Filter by licence and velocity layers.
  2. Tap ↓ on a kit; it downloads in your browser. Then import the file with ↑ as above.

Freesound with your own key

600,000+ free sounds. About two minutes to set up, and the key never leaves your phone.

  1. Kits → Get more sounds → GET MORE SOUNDS → SET UP FREESOUND.
  2. CREATE FREESOUND ACCOUNT, or I ALREADY HAVE ONE.
  3. Open the key form; copy the three values the app shows into it.
  4. Back in the app, Paste the key, then CHECK KEY. A green “Key works” means you're done.
  5. Now search per kit slot (Snare suggests “snare one shot”), ▶ preview, and Use to fill the slot. CC0 only by default; switch on CC-BY if you like. Credits are written for you.

Non-commercial sounds are never offered. Remove the key any time in Settings → Freesound.

Output presets, split output, Bluetooth

Sound right on headphones, a phone speaker or a PA.

  1. Settings → Audio & output.
  2. Pick a preset: Headphones, Phone speaker, Small BT or PA / amp. Then tweak low-cut, EQ and the gentle bus compressor. A limiter is always on to protect the PA.
  3. Split: drums L · click R sends the click to one ear for in-ear monitoring.
  4. On a Bluetooth speaker the app warns once about delay; Bluetooth delay compensation lines up Band view and your taps.
